The product

Every project begins with a contract. Every process should too.

Deedline AI gives project teams one connected environment to understand agreements, administer commercial workflows, and protect contractual rights and obligations.

One connected foundation

From static documents to a living contract model.

Deedline structures contracts into reusable Digital Contract Elements™ and connects them with the records created throughout project delivery. Every agreement, event, decision, and supporting document stays linked to its contractual context.

Digital Contract Model™
A shared commercial foundation connecting prime contracts, downstream agreements, correspondence, changes, schedules, payments, risks, and claims.
