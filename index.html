The problem

Contract value leaks through disconnected work.

Project teams negotiate in documents, communicate in inboxes, plan in schedules, approve in workflows, and defend decisions months later. The commercial story fragments across tools—and the cost appears when it is hardest to reverse.

01

Terms become static files

Rights and obligations are difficult to connect to the events that activate them.

02

Project systems stay isolated

Schedule, cost, correspondence, and contract records tell partial versions of the same story.

03

Teams react after exposure compounds

Evidence is reconstructed, approvals slow down, and commercial leverage erodes.

The platform thesis

Make the contract an active operating system.

Deedline connects the legal, commercial, and operational layers of project delivery so teams can move from document storage to decision intelligence.

LAYER 01

Contract intelligence

Transform agreements into structured clauses, obligations, approvals, dependencies, and risk signals that teams can trace.

LAYER 02

Commercial operations

Connect change orders, claims, correspondence, schedules, payment applications, risk, and negotiation workflows.

LAYER 03

Governed collaboration

Coordinate internal teams and outside counsel with scoped access, controlled data residency, retention, and audit-ready records.
